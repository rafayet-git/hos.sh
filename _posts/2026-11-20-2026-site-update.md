### Assets Page

I finally have a fully functioning [assets page](https://hos.sh/assets/)! It is inspired by the open directories that you might sometimes find on websites (such as with a [search tool](https://opendirsearch.abifog.com/)), as I enjoy exploring for random hidden treasures as an archivist. Normally, having such a list could be a security risk for most websites, but in my case I just wanted easy access to my `/assets` folder, containing stuff that I have no problem sharing. 

Implementing it (in terms of what needed modifying) was straightforward. I needed to create a [custom Ruby plugin][plugin-link] that would automatically generate the assets page for each directory/subdirectory. It's in a graph view that shows each file's size and last modified date (based on git history). Aside from a custom CSS file, I did not need to make any other changes in the source code, as Jekyll will automatically run and build anything that is on the `_plugins` folder.

### Mermaid 

To prepare for upcoming posts, I wanted a way to display architectural diagrams natively without relying too much on paint tools. I haven't used Mermaid before, but from what I've seen, it could be perfect to just embed graphs and diagrams directly into my site.
